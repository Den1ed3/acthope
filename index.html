Let’s talk it through ↗
Where can sessions take place?+

Online, at your home in Melbourne, or at a mutually agreed in-person location. The suburb, privacy, accessibility and suitability of the setting are discussed before an appointment is confirmed. There is no walk-in clinic address advertised at this stage.

Do I need a referral to enquire?+

You can make an initial enquiry directly. Any documentation needed for your circumstances or intended funding arrangement will be discussed before booking.

What happens at the first appointment?+

We start by listening: your concerns, your strengths and what you would like to be easier in everyday life. Alea then discusses appropriate assessment, goals and possible next steps with you.

Can a parent or support person join?+

Yes. Where helpful and with your agreement, a parent, carer or support person can join. Their involvement is planned around the person receiving support.

Can I use NDIS, Medicare or private health funding?+

Funding and rebate eligibility have not been confirmed for this preview. Please ask before booking and do not assume that a session will be claimable.

How do I book?+

Choose your preferred session format on the enquiry page. In this preview, you can review your details and open an email draft. No appointment is reserved or confirmed through the website.
