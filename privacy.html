How this website preview handles your enquiry.

Enquiry details

This static preview does not send form entries to a server or save them to browser storage. Details stay in the open page until you leave or reload it. Choosing “Open email draft” passes the entered details to your email application. You choose whether to send that email.

Keep health information private

Please do not include medical reports, diagnoses or other sensitive health information in a website enquiry. A suitable intake process can be discussed directly with the clinician.

Hosting and images

The hosting provider may process standard connection logs to deliver the website. The site itself contains no analytics or advertising scripts. Imagery is illustrative stock photography and does not depict the clinician or actual clients.

Before the clinic website launches

This is a design preview. Fees, service availability, Australian professional status, funding eligibility and policies must be confirmed by the clinic. A complete privacy policy and booking terms will be needed for the final operational service.
