ALEA CLARISSE T. TOLEDO

Grounded in care.
Guided by your goals.

Alea completed her Bachelor of Science in Speech-Language Pathology at De La Salle Medical and Health Sciences Institute in 2024, graduating cum laude. She passed the Philippine Speech-Language Pathology Licensure Examination in November 2024.

Her résumé includes clinical work at Kids in Motion Therapy Center, Stride Center and Playful Paths, alongside freelance teletherapy experience from February 2025.

Her training has included work with children and adults, speech and language needs, social and cognitive communication, and augmentative and alternative communication (AAC).
