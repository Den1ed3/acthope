ABOUT YOUR SUPPORT

Before your
first session.

How do I know which service to choose?+

You do not need to choose a diagnosis or know the right clinical term. Describe what you would like support with, and Alea can discuss the next step and whether she can meet your needs.

Is online support suitable for everyone?+

Online suitability depends on the person, the goals, access to technology and the support available during sessions. This is discussed before a booking is confirmed.

Will I receive strategies to use between sessions?+

Where appropriate, sessions include practical strategies that you or your support people can use in everyday routines. The approach is agreed together.

Do you offer swallowing or feeding appointments?+

Swallowing and feeding appointments are not offered through this preview. Please enquire about current scope and suitability rather than booking these needs as a general communication session.

Where can sessions take place?+

Online, at your home in Melbourne, or at a mutually agreed in-person location. The suburb, privacy, accessibility and suitability of the setting are discussed before an appointment is confirmed. There is no walk-in clinic address advertised at this stage.

Do I need a referral to enquire?+

You can make an initial enquiry directly. Any documentation needed for your circumstances or intended funding arrangement will be discussed before booking.
